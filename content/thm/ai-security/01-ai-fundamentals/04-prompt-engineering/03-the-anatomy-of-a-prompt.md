# the anatomy of a prompt

> Effective AI prompts are carefully structured instructions, good prompt explicitly spells out what you want, how you want it, and any constraints to follow, an effective prompt can be broken down into 4 clear components:

- instruction (task to perform)
- context (background information)
- output format (structure/style)
- constraints (rules or limits)

> these restrictions/components help to get a more reliable result form the LLMs

## Four Pillars of Effective Prompts

> Instruction (task)

- this is the core command or action you want from the AI
- expressed with a clear verb
- clear instructions set expectations and direct the AI's focus

> Context (background)

- context provides the AI with relevant information or a scenario so it understands the situation and perspective
- this can include domain details, objectives, or background documents
- such context steers the AI's tone and content
- the more relevant background you supply, the less guessing the model has to do, reducing errors
- external context could be useful too

> Output format (structure)

- specify how you want the answer to look
- this could mean asking for bullet points, a numbered list, a table, code blocks or a certain word count
- explicitly stating format and length makes the response immediately useful

> Constraints (boundaries)

- these are any rules or limits you impose on the response
- constraints guide the model to follow specific boundaries
- forbidding certain topics
- enforcing a style guide
- mandating a tone

## Specificity vs Verbosity

> Clear, specific prompts generally yield better results, but overly wordy prompts confuse the model, don't overload the prompt with extraneous detail or multiple tasks mixed together, this can also cause the model to hallucinate or give an unfocused answer, provide enough detail to remove ambiguity, while keeping the prompt concise

## Engineered Success

> well instructed prompts can make the difference between reliable result and hallucinated or directionless results
