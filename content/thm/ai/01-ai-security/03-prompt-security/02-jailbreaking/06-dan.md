# DAN

> DAN ("Do Anything Now") was a roleplay prompt from December 2022 that got ChatGPT to act as a character with no safety rules. Reddit users kept refining it as OpenAI patched each version, sparking an arms race that ended with the classic prompts defunct by late 2023, but not before it drew serious attention from AI safety researchers and showed how community experimentation can expose real security flaws.
