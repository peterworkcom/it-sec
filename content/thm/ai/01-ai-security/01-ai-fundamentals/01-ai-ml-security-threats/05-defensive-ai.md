# defensive AI

> AI can speed up and enhance

- our ability to analyze
- our ability to predict
- our ability to summarize/digest
- our ability to investigate

## secure AI

- `securing AI models`: preventing these kinds of attacks is to secure the models thyself, the use of `RBAC` (Role-Based Access Control) and `MFA` (Multi-Factor Authentication) can help restrict access and add an extra layer of security to AI systems

- `privacy protection`: the training data can sometimes contain confidential or sensitive information, such as patient records, training data should be treated as any other sensitive data and encrypted

- `implementation of AI security standards`: the implementation of well-established standards and frameworks can proactively address potential risks

- `model monitoring`: on model's performance drops and flagging when it needs to be retrained, monitoring should also detect unexpected behaviors, biases, or anomalies that may indicate a security attack, this can be done using "explainability tools" like `SHAP` and `LIME`
